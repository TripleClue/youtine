Questions

Things people ask.

What is Youtine?

Youtine is a family organiser app built around reducing mental load - the invisible cognitive work of keeping a household running. It brings routines, shared tasks, and everyday plans into one calm, shared space. It's not a productivity app. It's a warm place to hold the things that usually live in one person's head.

Is Youtine free?

Yes - Youtine is free to download and use. A premium subscription unlocks additional features including unlimited family members, full routine customisation, and priority notifications. There's a 14-day free trial on premium, with no credit card required.

How is Youtine different from a shared calendar?

A shared calendar shows you what's happening. Youtine holds everything that makes it happen - the routines, the daily tasks, the check-ins that confirm things actually got done. It's less about scheduling and more about sharing the invisible work underneath the schedule.

What does "mental load" mean?

Mental load refers to the invisible cognitive work of managing a household - keeping track of appointments, school forms, shopping, routines, birthdays, and the thousand small things that need to happen. Research from the University of Bath and University of Melbourne shows that 71% of this invisible mental work falls on one person in most households. Youtine is built to help share it.

Can I use Youtine with my co-parent, housemate, or extended family?

Yes. Youtine works for any household sharing plans and responsibilities — couples with or without children, co-parents, multi-generational families, and roommates. If you share a home and an invisible workload, Youtine was made for you.
