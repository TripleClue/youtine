Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1 Introduction

This Cookie Policy covers the Youtine website (youtine.app). The Youtine mobile app operates separately — it uses device identifiers and Mixpanel for in-app analytics, which are described in our Privacy Policy.

The website uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies to keep it working and to understand how visitors use it, so we can improve their experience.

2 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit — such as your preferences or whether you've already seen a consent notice.

4 What We Don't Do

  • We do not use cookies for advertising or retargeting
  • We do not sell cookie data to third parties
  • We do not use cookies to build personal profiles

5 Managing Your Preferences

You can update your cookie preferences at any time using the button below. Changes take effect immediately.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies — note that doing so may affect how the site works. For guidance, visit aboutcookies.org.

6 The Youtine App

The Youtine mobile app does not use browser cookies. It uses device identifiers and Mixpanel to collect anonymised usage analytics within the app. You can opt out of in-app analytics at any time via Privacy Settings in the app. This is described fully in our Privacy Policy.

7 Contact

For questions about cookies or your privacy, contact us at privacy@youtine.com, or submit a complaint to the Dutch Data Protection Authority at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.
