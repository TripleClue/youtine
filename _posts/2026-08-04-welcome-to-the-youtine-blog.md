We started Youtine because of a quiet, familiar feeling: that one person in a household is usually holding everything in their head. The appointments, the forms, the shopping, the birthdays, the sports kit. The remembering.

This blog is where we'll write about that (the **mental load**) and the small, practical ways families can share it.

## What you can expect here

We're not going to add to your to-do list. Expect short, honest pieces on things like:

- How the mental load actually gets divided (and why it so often doesn't)
- Gentle routines that hold up on the hard days
- What we're learning as we build Youtine

## Why it matters

Research from the University of Bath and University of Melbourne found that **71% of household mental-load tasks fall on one person**. That imbalance is invisible, which is exactly what makes it hard to fix.

Naming it is the first step. Sharing it is the next.

> Share the mental load, not just the tasks.
